About

MirageGrid is a local node-mesh VPN. A circuit is an entry peer plus hops across the 25-peer mesh. The optional SOCKS5 proxy listens on 127.0.0.1:1080.

Cap-7 is the mesh DNS factory: four hub mirrors and three decoys. It keeps those names on the mesh. It does not register names at a public ICANN registrar.
